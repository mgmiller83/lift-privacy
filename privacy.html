LIFT Gym · Household Strength & Hypertrophy

Privacy Policy & Health Connect Disclosure

Package: com.lift.householdgym · Last updated: May 2026

Local-First Guarantee: LIFT stores your workout history, strength baselines, body weight measurements, profiles, and basement gym equipment strictly on your device using local IndexedDB and browser storage. The developer operates no analytics servers, no advertising SDKs, and no central backend database collecting your personal data.

1. Android Health Connect Permissions (Write-Only)

LIFT integrates optionally with Android Health Connect on your device so completed workouts can appear in Google Fit, Fitbit, or Strava. LIFT uses write-only permissions and does not request or read health records written by other apps:

  • Write Exercise Sessions (WRITE_EXERCISE): Saves workout start/end times, exercise type, workout title, and set summary notes.
  • Write Total Calories Burned (WRITE_TOTAL_CALORIES_BURNED): Saves estimated workout calorie expenditure computed from session work or heart rate.
  • Write Heart Rate Samples (WRITE_HEART_RATE): Saves optional heart-rate time-series points recorded during your session via a paired Bluetooth heart-rate wearable or manual summary.

Health Connect records are written directly to Android's encrypted on-device Health Connect store only when initiated or enabled by you. LIFT never sells, rents, or uses Health Connect data for advertising. You may revoke Health Connect permissions at any time in Android Settings → Security & privacy → Health Connect → App permissions.

2. Bluetooth Heart Rate Monitor (neverForLocation)

If you connect a smartwatch or chest strap over Bluetooth Low Energy (GATT Heart Rate Profile 0x180D), LIFT requests nearby Bluetooth scan/connect permissions strictly with the neverForLocation declaration (location permission is capped at Android 11 / API 30 and disabled on Android 12+). LIFT never tracks or stores GPS coordinates or physical location.

3. Foreground Rest Timer & Exact Alarms

While a rest countdown runs between sets, LIFT uses an Android foreground service notification (specialUse) and optional exact-alarm scheduling so rest alerts chime accurately even if you switch apps.

4. Optional User-Configured External Connections

All third-party integrations are disabled by default and transmit data over HTTPS directly to services you explicitly configure:

  • Google Gemini AI Coach (Optional): Uses your own Gemini API key stored locally to generate workouts or training debriefs.
  • Strava (Optional): Uses your own Strava OAuth credentials to upload completed workout activities directly to your Strava account.
  • Google Sheets (Optional): Sends workout rows directly to your personal Google Apps Script web app URL.
  • Household Backup Vault (Optional): Syncs profile backups directly to your own Supabase or custom HTTPS webhook endpoint (secret OAuth and API tokens are excluded from exported vault backups).
  • Exercise Guide Clips (Optional): Opening an instructional clip loads privacy-enhanced YouTube embeds (youtube-nocookie.com) or external search links only upon tap.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Because your training ledger resides locally on your device, you have complete control: you can export JSON/CSV backups, delete any athlete profile or workout inside LIFT, revoke external integration permissions in the Sync tab or Android System Settings, or uninstall LIFT / clear app storage to permanently erase all local records.
